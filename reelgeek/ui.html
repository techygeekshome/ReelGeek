Before you post

  • Add trending audio in the app, not here. Native sound gets better distribution than baked-in music, and it keeps you clear of copyright claims. The cuts sit on a clean tempo grid so a track at a similar BPM will lock on.
  • The first second decides everything. Lead with your strongest photo and a hook line people have to resolve.
  • Post the same edit to both platforms — the 1080×1920 file works as-is for TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
  • Re-roll before you settle. Same photos, new seed, different edit. Pick the one that feels best.
Worth saying plainly: this controls the craft, not the algorithm. A good edit gives good photos their best chance — it can't manufacture reach on its own.
